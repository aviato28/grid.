# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- Caption studio: eight fonts, text colours and sizes, frame colours, editable date and location (prefilled from the photo), footer layouts and an orange film date stamp, per card or for the whole sheet.
- 14 new card shapes: Square, Wide, Classic, Wallet, Photo strip, Passport, Film strip, Postage stamp, Ticket stub, Postcard, Bookmark, Trading card, Slide mount and Contact sheet.
- Mixed sheets: different shapes on one page, packed in straight rows so a guillotine cuts them cleanly.
- 11 film filters (Gold, Portrait, Vivid, Superia, Instant, Tungsten, Disposable, Expired, Tri-X, Soft B&W, Sepia) with intensity, grain, vignette, light leak, brightness, contrast and warmth. Sheet-wide or per card, and printed into the PDF.
- Drafts: sheets save automatically with their own copy of the photos, so they survive deleting the originals. Rename, duplicate or delete them from Home.
- Drag to reorder cards.
- "Auto" frame colour and font, so shape defaults no longer block choosing white or a font sheet-wide.
- Much smaller app.
- Fixes: caption text no longer overlaps, dark-mode title and back arrow are readable, going back saves your edits, pinch zoom follows your fingers.

## 0.2.0
- Pick up to 90 photos and frame them as Mini instant-film cards.
- Pan and zoom each photo inside its frame, and add a handwritten caption.
- Live A4 preview, 9 cards per page, with automatic extra pages.
- Export a print-accurate PDF with crop marks and a size-check ruler, then share or save it.
- New app icon.

## 0.1.0
- First build: in-app updater, theme.
